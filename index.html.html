Arena Mode Competition Framework

Module: Multiplayer  ·  Status: Spec Complete

Arena Mode is a structured competitive layer where users engage in real-time and asynchronous forex news trading simulations against peers globally. It applies game-theory pressure, ranking mechanics, and performance-adjusted scoring to replicate institutional trading competition dynamics — turning passive learning into high-stakes, measurable skill validation.

Competition Types

📅
Weekly Themed Challenges

Single macroeconomic theme per week (e.g. "Central Bank Week"). All participants face the same synthetic news environment. Rankings reset weekly. Top 20% earn promotion points.

⚔
Head-to-Head Matches

Two players receive identical news scenarios simultaneously. First to hit +3% portfolio return without exceeding 5% drawdown wins. 10-minute time cap with live P&L streaming.

🏢
Team Trading Desks

Teams of 3–5 players split roles: analyst, execution, risk manager. Team score = sum of individual risk-adjusted returns. Roles rotate each round to ensure skill breadth.

Global Tournaments

Monthly 3-round eliminations. Bracket of 256 players. Each round introduces increased news complexity. Final round: multi-event simultaneous trading (NFP + FOMC overlap).

Scoring System

Total score is a weighted composite of five performance dimensions:

// Arena Score Formula

Total_Score = (Accuracy × 0.25) + (RiskAdj × 0.30) + (Consistency × 0.20) + (Execution × 0.15) − (DrawdownPenalty × 0.10)

// Risk-Adjusted Return (simplified Sharpe proxy)
RiskAdj = (Avg_Return − RiskFree_Rate) / Std_Dev_Returns

// Drawdown Penalty: deducted at 2x rate beyond 5% peak-to-trough
DrawdownPenalty = max(0, (MaxDD − 0.05) × 200)

Fairness & Anti-Gaming Rules

Anti-Exploitation Protocol
Volatility spikes above 3 standard deviations are flagged. Any trade placed within 50ms of news timestamp is subject to execution latency normalization — preventing algorithmic front-running in the simulation environment.
  • All participants receive news data at identically randomized delays (150ms–800ms window) to prevent reaction-time advantages.
  • Head-to-Head scenarios use cryptographic hash verification — same seed, same price path for both players.
  • Luck filter: outcomes are cross-validated against a 100-simulation Monte Carlo run. Wins with p-value < 0.15 are marked "luck-adjusted" and excluded from Elo calculation.
  • Spike guard: positions held < 5 seconds during >2% price moves are post-reviewed and may be voided for investigation.
  • Seasonal resets prevent Elo stagnation. Top-tier players are seeded into harder bracket pools each month.

Sandbox Mode Feature Specification

Module: Free Play  ·  Status: Spec Complete

Sandbox Mode is a consequence-free simulation environment providing unlimited access to historical and synthetic forex scenarios. It functions as the primary skill development layer — enabling deliberate practice, hypothesis testing, and post-trade reflection without ranking or P&L consequences. Users control all simulation parameters.

Core Features

Learning Tools

💡
Contextual Hint System

Three-tier hint structure. Tier 1: conceptual nudge (free). Tier 2: directional bias suggestion (costs XP). Tier 3: optimal trade suggestion with rationale (costs 2× XP). Hints are tracked and reduce scenario score to prevent over-reliance.

📈
Post-Trade Analytics

Full trade reconstruction: entry timing vs. optimal entry, max favorable/adverse excursion, Sharpe contribution per trade, comparison against median player performance on same scenario.

🔍
Error Breakdown Engine

Classifies each trade error into one of 8 categories: premature entry, overleverage, wrong direction, ignored signal, held too long, exited too early, correlation blindspot, or news misread. Each category links to targeted learning modules.
